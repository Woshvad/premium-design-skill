Retry policies

When an endpoint answers with a 5xx status or does not answer within 10 seconds, Tidewire retries the delivery. A policy sets how many attempts are made and how long Tidewire waits between them.

The default policy

New endpoints retry 8 times with exponential backoff, starting at 30 seconds and capped at 6 hours. The last attempt happens about 13 hours after the first.

{
  "attempts": 8,
  "backoff": "exponential",
  "initial_delay_s": 30,
  "max_delay_s": 21600
}

Fields

FieldTypeDescription
attemptsinteger, 1 to 20Deliveries after the first failure.
backofffixed or exponentialHow the delay grows between attempts.
initial_delay_sinteger secondsWait before the first retry.

Idempotency

Retries reuse the original Tidewire-Delivery-Id header, so your handler can drop a delivery it has already processed.
