Read from the station, not the zone

Marine forecasts cover a zone the size of a county. Ebbline reads the station closest to the pass you are crossing and shows when that water goes slack, how long the window lasts, and what the current does after it closes.

Predictions are stored on the phone, so the sheet still opens at the launch beach with no signal.

I used to plan Cattle Pass from three browser tabs. Now I check one sheet the night before.

Mara Lindqvist, sea kayak guide, Friday Harbor
