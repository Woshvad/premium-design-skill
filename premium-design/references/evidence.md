## Blind tests of the skill itself

The skill was tested the way it asks you to test pages: built, rendered and judged blind against a builder with no skill.

**Round 1** (3 briefs: perfumer, developer CLI, restaurant; 1 judge each). The first version, a 9,800-word rulebook, lost all three, 31-34 against 34-38 out of 50. It scored higher on not-AI and craft but lower on premium (5-6 against 8). Its builders optimised for compliance and novelty: a cockpit typeface, a cool palette on a warm brief, a timid display scale and a toy-like product drawing. The baselines won on presence, with giant names, coveted product renders and deep niche content, even though they used recognisable AI trope stacks.

**Round 2** (4 briefs: headphones, freelancer finance app, ceramics studio, developer CLI; 2 independent judges each, ranking four blind pages from full-page renders). The four pages were the no-skill baseline, the revised rulebook (V1), this lean presence-first entry point (V2) and a two-pass variant (V3). Means over 8 judgements:

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| No skill | 3.25 | 0 | 6.75 | 6.63 | 3.50 | 5.38 | 8.63 |
| V1 rulebook | 2.38 | 2 | 6.88 | 7.25 | 6.75 | 6.88 | 7.63 |
| **V2 presence-first (this SKILL.md)** | **2.13** | 2 | 6.88 | **7.75** | 6.63 | 6.88 | 8.50 |
